Aephate Pesticide

Acephate is a broad-spectrum, organophosphate insecticide used to control a wide range of chewing and sucking pests on crops like cotton, rice, and vegetables. It acts through both direct contact and by being absorbed systemically by the plant, and it works by inhibiting the enzyme acetylcholinesterase, which disrupts the nervous system of the insect. It is often sold in water-soluble powder or granular formulations that are mixed with water and applied as a spray. Know more [Buy Now]

Propoxur Pesticides

Propoxur is a carbamate insecticide that works as a reversible cholinesterase inhibitor to control a wide range of household, public health, and some agricultural pests. It provides a rapid "knockdown" effect and has long residual activity. Historically, it was widely used in products like the consumer brand Baygon, and in various formulations such as sprays, baits, powders, and pet flea collars. Ammonium Sulphate

Ammonium sulfate (\((NH_{4})_{2}SO_{4}\)) is an inorganic salt primarily used as a fertilizer, providing nitrogen and sulfur to plants. It is a white, water-soluble solid that is especially useful for alkaline soils because its application can lower the soil's pH. Beyond agriculture, it has applications in industries like pharmaceuticals, and in molecular biology for protein purification.Provides essential nutrients: It contains both nitrogen (about 21%) and sulfur (about 24%), which are crucial for plant growth, protein formation, and chlorophyll production. Red Sandalwood

Red sandalwood, or Pterocarpus santalinus, is a valuable, slow-growing hardwood native to southern India, prized for its red heartwood. It is used for fine furniture, musical instruments, and in traditional medicine, where it's known for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and cooling properties for the skin and for treating ailments like dysentery. Due to overharvesting for its high value, especially in international markets, the species is now endangered and its trade is heavily regulated, notes this Wikipedia article, a document from the Wildlife Crime Control Bureau, and a 1mg article.
